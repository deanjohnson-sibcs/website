About Si BCS

A small firm with deep expertise, on your side of the table

Si BCS exists to give finance and technology leaders straight, independent advice about their financial systems, and the hands-on skill to make the right thing happen.

Get in touch

Our story

Built around a simple frustration

Si BCS was founded by Simon Bell to give organisations truly independent EPM advice, free of the vendor margin that quietly shapes so much of the guidance in this market. He had seen too many organisations pick the wrong platform, or the right one for the wrong reasons.

The idea was straightforward. Give people truly independent advice, charge for the advice rather than the software, and stay honest about what things really cost.

In 2025, Ahtishaam Khan joined as Chief Technology Officer, adding deep technology leadership and the hands-on ability to deliver, not just recommend. The two had built Opal Wave together, a specialist SAP EPM and analytics practice acquired by Sapphire Systems, where Simon was founding director and Shaam co-founder and CTO/CIO. Today Si BCS sits between the office of the CFO and the office of the CIO, and works with a trusted network of senior specialists to deliver across both.

What we believe

The people

Who you actually work with

You deal with senior practitioners from day one, not a sales team who hand you over once the contract is signed.

SB

Simon Bell

Founder & Managing Director

Simon has spent nearly thirty years in enterprise performance management, from Hyperion Solutions in the late 1990s through founding and leading Opal Wave for fifteen years across SAP BPC, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP HANA, S/4HANA Group Reporting, OneStream and Azure. He founded Si BCS to give finance leaders that depth on a truly independent footing.

AK

Ahtishaam Khan

Chief Technology Officer

Shaam is a technology executive with nearly 30 years of international experience across enterprise systems, cloud, data and AI. He co-founded and was CTO/CIO of Opal Wave, the SAP EPM and analytics specialist later acquired by Sapphire Systems, and began his career delivering SAP BPC (Outlooksoft) and CODA. At Si BCS he leads technology strategy and delivery, from solution architecture and secure cloud to data platforms and responsible AI, making sure clients own robust systems that work.

Our wider team

Behind Simon and Ahtishaam sits a network of expert solution architects and senior technical and functional specialists we bring in for delivery. It means a small firm can staff a serious programme without ever losing the senior attention that made you choose us.
