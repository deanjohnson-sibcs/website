About Si BCS

A boutique firm with deep expertise, on your side of the table

Si BCS exists to give finance and technology leaders straight, independent advice about their financial systems, and the hands-on skill to make the right thing happen.

Get in touch

Our story

Built around a simple frustration

Si BCS was founded by Simon Bell to give organisations independent EPM advice, driven by what the client needs rather than what a vendor is trying to sell. He had seen too many organisations pick the wrong platform, or the right one for the wrong reasons.

The idea was straightforward: give people advice they can trust, be transparent about any interest before they decide, and stay honest about what things really cost.

In 2025, Ahtishaam Khan joined Simon as a partner, adding deep technology leadership and the hands-on ability to deliver, not just recommend. The two had built Opal Wave together, a specialist SAP EPM and analytics practice acquired by Sapphire Systems, now part of NTT DATA, where Simon was founding director and Shaam co-founder and CTO/CIO. Today Si BCS sits between the office of the CFO and the office of the CIO, and works with a trusted network of senior specialists to deliver across both.

The people

Who you actually work with

You deal with senior practitioners from day one, not a sales team who hand you over once the contract is signed.

Simon Bell

Founder & Managing Director

Simon has spent nearly thirty years in enterprise performance management. He began at Hyperion Solutions, the original EPM pioneer, then led the independent EPM analyst and advisory firm BPM Partners, before founding and running Opal Wave for fifteen years, the specialist SAP EPM practice acquired by Sapphire Systems, now part of NTT DATA. He founded Si BCS to give finance leaders that depth on an independent footing, where any interest is disclosed before you decide. Today he leads its financial performance side, from EPM platform selection and business cases to FP&A, consolidation and close.

Ahtishaam Khan

Partner & CTO

Shaam is a technology executive with nearly thirty years of international experience across enterprise systems, cloud, data and AI. He began in enterprise finance and business intelligence with CODA, went on to work at the technical origins of SAP BPC through OutlookSoft, then co-founded Opal Wave and served as its CTO and CIO, the SAP EPM and analytics specialist acquired by Sapphire Systems, now part of NTT DATA. At Si BCS he leads technology strategy and delivery, from solution architecture and secure cloud to modern data platforms and the responsible use of AI, so clients own robust systems that work.
