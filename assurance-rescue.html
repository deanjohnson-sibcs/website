EPM Assurance & Rescue

Keep your EPM programme controlled and moving

When a programme is under pressure, more status reporting rarely fixes it. We give sponsors an independent view of scope, design, delivery risk and readiness, then help turn that view into practical action.

Request a programme review

When to involve us

The pressure points we are built for

Assurance works best before a wobble becomes a failure. These are the moments sponsors bring us in.

Confidence is slipping

The programme reports progress, but confidence in scope, design or readiness is falling.

Finance and IT diverging

The two sides are working to different assumptions, and the gap is widening.

The partner needs challenge

The implementation partner needs independent challenge, or clearer governance around it.

Risk surfacing late

Testing, data, integration or cutover risk is becoming visible later than it should.

A programme has stalled

A stalled or failing programme needs a recovery plan sponsors can actually act on.

You want a client-side eye

You want senior client-side expertise alongside the delivery partner from the outset.

What we review

Where the risk actually hides

We look past the status deck to the things that decide whether a programme lands: the outcome, the design, the plan, and whether anyone owns the decisions.

The review lens

Six places we look first

  • Business outcome and scope integrity
  • Requirements, architecture and design decisions
  • Plan, resourcing, dependencies and governance
  • Data, integration, testing and operational readiness
  • Commercial assumptions and change exposure
  • Decision ownership and stakeholder confidence

Our approach

From an independent view to controlled action

Three ways in, depending on how much pressure the programme is under.

Programme assurance

Periodic or continuous client-side assurance. We challenge material decisions and make risks visible to sponsors before they become delivery failures.

Health check

A focused assessment that finds the few issues most likely to threaten the outcome, with evidence and accountable actions.

Rescue and recovery

Where delivery has lost control, we help stabilise it, agree the true status, reset priorities and set a credible recovery plan.

Starting engagement

EPM Programme Health Check

A short, fixed-scope diagnostic that gives sponsors an independent view of programme health, the material risks, the decisions required, and the immediate assurance or recovery plan. Scope and duration are agreed after an initial conversation.
