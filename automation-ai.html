AI and automation that earn their place

There is a lot of noise about AI right now. We help you find the work that is actually worth automating, then build it properly, from simple rule-based workflows through to Copilots and autonomous agents.

Three levels of automation

Match the technology to the task

Not everything needs AI, and some things need more than a chatbot. We help you place each process at the right level, and build it properly.

01

Rule-based automation

Deterministic, high-volume workflows automated with Power Automate, Logic Apps and RPA, reliable, auditable, and fast to deliver.

02

AI-assisted workflows

Copilots and custom assistants that draft, summarise, classify and support your team on judgement-adjacent work.

03

Agentic AI

Autonomous agents that don't just answer, they take action across your systems, safely and within guardrails you control.

In finance, that means

Where AI earns its keep in finance

The applications we see actually pay off, built on trustworthy financial data and kept under human control.

Predictive planning & forecasting

Machine-learning forecasts of demand, headcount and cost that feed straight into your plan, so forecasts are grounded in data rather than gut feel.

Scenario and what-if at speed

Generate, compare and stress-test scenarios in minutes, giving leadership real options before a decision is made.

Automated variance explanations

AI drafts the narrative behind the numbers, the why behind a movement, for management and board reporting, with a human check before it ships.

Anomaly detection in the close

Flag reconciling items, outliers and control breaks early, so the close is faster, cleaner and easier to trust.
