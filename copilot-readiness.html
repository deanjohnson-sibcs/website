Microsoft 365 Copilot

Is your SharePoint ready for Copilot?

Before you switch on Microsoft Copilot for your team, make sure your SharePoint environment is clean, secure, and sharing the right things with the right people.

Get in touch
73% of organisations have oversharing issues before Copilot rollout

The risk of rushing in: Copilot surfaces content from across your Microsoft 365 environment. If permissions aren't right, employees could see confidential files, HR records, or sensitive data they were never meant to access — instantly, at scale.

What we review
A thorough check across your SharePoint environment

Our review covers every area that Copilot will draw on — so you can roll it out with confidence, not crossed fingers.

Sharing permissions audit

We identify files, folders, and sites shared too broadly — including "Anyone with the link" links and legacy permissions that have built up over time.

Tenant & site settings review

We check your SharePoint and OneDrive sharing settings against Microsoft best practice, flagging anything that could expose data unexpectedly.

Guest & external access check

We map out who outside your organisation has access to what — and whether those permissions are still appropriate or have been forgotten.

Sensitive content identification

We highlight high-risk areas — such as HR folders, finance documents, or executive files — and verify they are appropriately protected before Copilot goes live.

Copilot readiness scoring

We assess your environment against Microsoft's own Copilot readiness criteria, giving you a clear picture of where you stand today.

Best practice configuration

We review your information architecture, labelling, and governance settings to ensure Copilot can work effectively without surfacing the wrong content.

Why this matters now

Copilot doesn't respect permissions that shouldn't exist

Microsoft Copilot is incredibly powerful — but it works with whatever your users can already access. If permissions have crept over the years, Copilot will surface that content too. Cleaning this up before rollout protects your people and your business.

Employees accessing sensitive HR or payroll files they were never meant to see

Former employees or external guests with lingering access to confidential content

Anonymous sharing links that expose documents to anyone on the internet

Compliance and regulatory risk if sensitive data is surfaced without appropriate controls
