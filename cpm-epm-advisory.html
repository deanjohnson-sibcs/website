What is CPM / EPM?

Planning, consolidation and reporting, unified

Corporate and Enterprise Performance Management platforms replace fragile spreadsheets with governed systems for financial and operational management. Done well, they shorten the close, sharpen the forecast, connect financial and operational planning (xP&A), and give leadership one version of the truth.

Financial planning & budgeting

Driver-based models, rolling forecasts and scenario planning that replace static spreadsheet exercises.

Financial consolidation

Automated consolidation of entities, eliminations and currency translation, cutting the close from weeks to days.

Management reporting

Consistent, governed P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and KPI reporting with transaction drill-through.

Scenario & what-if modelling

Model multiple futures so leadership can see the impact of decisions before committing to them.

Workforce planning

Headcount and salary modelling integrated directly with your financial forecast.

Regulatory & ESG reporting

Structured data collection with audit trails for statutory, regulatory and ESG submissions.

The selection problem

Choosing the wrong platform is an expensive mistake

With six or more credible EPM platforms on the market, each with different strengths, pricing models and implementation complexity, the wrong choice costs years and hundreds of thousands of pounds. Most organisations don't have the internal, cross-platform expertise to evaluate them objectively. That's exactly what we provide.

We hold no vendor partnerships that bias us, earn no referral fees, and take no licence margin. The recommendation you get is source-system agnostic and fits your requirements, your data and your budget, never a platform we're paid to push, and never lock-in for its own sake.

How we evaluate

Every platform, the same scorecard

We weight each dimension to what matters for you, then score every candidate the same way.

  • Functional fit to your actual requirements
  • Consolidation, close and reporting depth
  • Planning and forecasting capability
  • Data and ERP integration
  • Total cost of ownership, five years out
  • Implementation complexity and risk
  • Vendor viability and product roadmap
  • Fit with your team's skills and appetite

The platform landscape

There is no single best platform, only the best fit

The market falls into a handful of categories, each strong for different reasons. We know them all, and we match your requirements to the right category before we ever name a product.

Unified close & consolidation

Platforms built first for fast, controlled consolidation, with planning and reporting on the same engine. Often the right answer when the close is the pain.

Connected planning

Cloud-native, multidimensional platforms for flexible, large-scale planning across finance, sales and supply chain.

ERP-native EPM suites

EPM that integrates deeply with a particular ERP, for organisations standardised on one vendor who want native reporting and consolidation.

Combined BI & planning

Platforms that merge business intelligence with planning and predictive analytics, often no-code, for teams that want self-service.

High-performance engines

Highly flexible, multidimensional calculation engines for complex, calculation-heavy models and bespoke logic.

Cloud analytics & planning

Modern SaaS platforms combining planning, analytics and reporting, for organisations moving off spreadsheets and legacy tools.

We stay current across every credible product in these categories. Which one we recommend depends entirely on you, never on us.
