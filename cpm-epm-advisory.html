What is CPM / EPM?
Planning, consolidation, and reporting — unified

Corporate Performance Management (CPM) and Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) platforms replace disconnected spreadsheets and manual processes with a single, governed system for financial and operational planning, consolidation, and reporting.

Financial planning & budgeting

Driver-based budgets, rolling forecasts, and scenario modelling — replacing static annual spreadsheet exercises with dynamic, connected planning.

Financial consolidation

Automated consolidation of legal entities, eliminations, currency translation, and minority interests — reducing close times from weeks to days.

Management reporting

Consistent, governed reporting across the business — P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and operational KPIs — with drill-through to transaction detail.

Scenario & what-if modelling

Model multiple futures instantly — best case, worst case, and everything in between — so leadership can make decisions with a clear view of the range of outcomes.

Workforce planning

Headcount planning, salary modelling, and HR cost integration — connecting people plans to financial forecasts in a single environment.

Regulatory & ESG reporting

Structured data collection, audit trails, and disclosure management for statutory reporting, ESG frameworks, and regulatory submissions.

The platform selection problem

With six or more credible EPM platforms on the market — each with different strengths, pricing models, and implementation complexity — choosing the wrong one is an expensive mistake. Most organisations don't have the in-house experience to evaluate them objectively. That's where independent advisory makes the difference.

Financial close & consolidation
OneStream Software

OneStream XF

A unified platform combining financial consolidation, planning, reporting, and analytics in a single application — eliminating the need for multiple point solutions and complex integrations.

  • Unified platform — consolidation and planning in one
  • Extensible marketplace of pre-built solutions
  • Strong for complex group consolidations
  • Cloud and on-premise deployment options
Best fit: Mid-market to enterprise organisations needing consolidation and planning in a single platform, particularly those replacing Hyperion or HFM.
Connected planning
Anaplan

Anaplan

A cloud-native connected planning platform built for large, complex organisations running multidimensional planning models across finance, sales, supply chain, and HR.

  • Highly flexible modelling — complex hierarchies and drivers
  • Strong for cross-functional connected planning
  • Enterprise scalability — thousands of concurrent users
  • Native AI and ML forecasting capabilities
Best fit: Large enterprises with complex, multi-dimensional planning requirements spanning finance and operations — particularly strong for sales and supply chain planning.
Oracle Cloud
Oracle

Oracle EPM Cloud

Oracle's suite of cloud EPM applications — including EPBCS for planning, FCCS for consolidation, and ARCS for account reconciliation — delivered on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

  • Deep ERP integration with Oracle Fusion and EBS
  • Mature, feature-rich consolidation (FCCS)
  • Strong regulatory and statutory reporting
  • Pre-built financial intelligence and narratives
Best fit: Organisations running Oracle ERP looking for tight native integration, or those with mature consolidation and statutory reporting requirements.
SAP ecosystem
SAP

SAP EPM / Group Reporting

SAP's EPM portfolio spans SAP Analytics Cloud for planning, SAP Group Reporting for statutory consolidation, and SAP BPC for organisations on legacy SAP environments.

  • Native integration with SAP S/4HANA and ECC
  • SAP Analytics Cloud — planning and BI in one tool
  • Group Reporting embedded in S/4HANA
  • Strong for SAP-centric organisations
Best fit: Organisations with significant SAP ERP investment seeking native EPM integration, or those on S/4HANA looking to leverage embedded Group Reporting.
Integrated business planning
Board International

Board

A unified decision-making platform combining business intelligence, planning, and predictive analytics — with a no-code modelling environment and strong self-service capabilities.

  • Combined BI and planning in a single platform
  • No-code modelling — lower IT dependency
  • Strong retail, manufacturing, and financial services templates
  • Flexible deployment — cloud, on-premise, hybrid
Best fit: Mid-market organisations wanting combined BI and planning with strong self-service, particularly in retail, manufacturing, and financial services.
IBM
IBM

IBM Planning Analytics (TM1)

IBM Planning Analytics, powered by the TM1 engine, is a high-performance multidimensional planning platform with extensive calculation power and a large installed base across financial services and insurance.

  • Extremely powerful calculation engine (TM1)
  • Highly flexible and customisable modelling
  • Large existing install base — migration experience
  • Strong in financial services and insurance
Best fit: Organisations with complex calculation requirements or an existing TM1 investment looking to modernise to Planning Analytics with Workspace.
