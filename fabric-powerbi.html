The challenge
Sound familiar?

Most organisations struggle with the same data problems. Microsoft Fabric and Power BI are built to solve them.

Data scattered everywhere

Reports pulling from different systems, spreadsheets, and databases — with no single version of the truth.

Slow, manual reporting

Teams spending hours each week preparing data instead of analysing it and making decisions.

Reports nobody trusts

Different departments running the same numbers and getting different answers — eroding confidence in the data.

No real-time visibility

Leaders making decisions based on last week's data, when the business is moving faster than the reports.

Microsoft Fabric and Power BI fix this — by bringing all your data into one governed lake, transforming it automatically, and surfacing it in AI-powered reports your whole organisation can rely on.

The platform
From raw data to business insight — in one connected platform

Microsoft Fabric unifies every stage of the data journey. No more stitching together separate tools. No more broken pipelines. Just one platform, end to end.

Layer 1 — Store

OneLake — your single source of truth

All your data — from every system, source, and application — lands in one governed data lake. No copies, no silos, no reconciliation.

Unified data governance

Security, access controls, and data lineage are applied once and enforced everywhere — so the right people see the right data.

Connect any source

ERP systems, CRM platforms, databases, files, cloud services — Fabric connects to them all without complex integration work.

Layer 2 — Transform

Data pipelines & integration

Automated pipelines move, clean, and transform your data on a schedule — so your reports always reflect the latest position, not a snapshot from last week.

Data warehouse & lakehouse

Fabric's built-in warehouse and lakehouse capabilities let you model data at scale — combining the flexibility of a lake with the structure of a warehouse.

AI-assisted data preparation

Copilot in Fabric helps your team clean, shape, and prepare data faster — reducing the manual work that slows down insight delivery.

Layer 3 — Report

Power BI — reports everyone can use

Beautiful, interactive dashboards and reports built on your governed data — accessible to decision-makers across the business, on any device.

Real-time & scheduled refresh

Reports update automatically as your data changes — giving leaders up-to-the-minute visibility without anyone having to run an export.

Copilot for Power BI

Ask questions in plain English and get instant answers, summaries, and visuals — no SQL, no technical skills required.

Business benefits
What this means for your organisation

The technology is powerful — but these are the outcomes your business will actually feel.

One version of the truth

With all data flowing through a single governed lake, every report, every team, and every decision is working from the same numbers — no more conflicting figures.

Faster, more confident decisions

When reports update in real time and leaders can query their data in plain English, decisions happen faster — and with far more confidence than gut feel allows.

Hours saved on manual reporting

Automated pipelines and scheduled refreshes eliminate the weekly grind of pulling data, building spreadsheets, and chasing numbers. Your team can focus on insight, not preparation.

Data you can trust and govern

Fabric's unified security and governance means you always know where your data is, who can see it, and where it came from — critical for compliance and audit.

Self-service for the whole business

Power BI puts insight directly into the hands of non-technical users. Finance, operations, sales, HR — everyone can explore their data without raising a ticket.

A platform that scales with you

Built on Azure, Fabric scales as your data volumes and user numbers grow — without the cost and complexity of managing infrastructure yourself.

AI capabilities
Intelligence built into every layer

Microsoft Fabric and Power BI aren't just data tools — they're AI-powered platforms that help your team work smarter, surface answers faster, and spot things they'd otherwise miss.

Copilot in Power BI

Ask questions in plain English — "What were our top-selling products last quarter?" — and get instant answers, charts, and summaries without writing a single formula.

Copilot in Fabric

Generate data pipelines, write transformations, and prepare datasets using natural language — dramatically reducing the time from raw data to clean, usable insight.

Smart narratives

Power BI automatically generates written summaries of your data — so non-technical audiences get clear, plain-English explanations alongside every chart.

Anomaly detection

AI continuously monitors your data and flags unusual patterns — a sudden drop in revenue, a spike in support tickets — before they become problems.

Forecasting

Built-in forecasting models project trends forward based on your historical data — giving planners and finance teams a reliable starting point for budgeting and strategy.

Key influencers

Power BI's AI visuals automatically identify what's driving a metric up or down — so you understand the why behind the numbers, not just the what.
