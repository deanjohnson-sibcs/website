Secure Access

Governed access

Your financial data has too many doors

EPM platforms, consolidation models and board reporting hold some of the most sensitive data in the business. Yet finance teams, auditors, implementation partners, VARs and offshore staff all need to reach it, and the usual answers, VPNs, emailed extracts and local copies, quietly scatter that data across devices you don't control.

A secure workspace closes those doors. People work inside a governed environment on your own Azure tenant. They can do their job, but the data stays put. Built on Azure Virtual Desktop and Nerdio.

Who it is for

  • Finance & FP&A teamsDay-to-day access to EPM and reporting, from any location or device.
  • External auditorsTime-boxed, read-only access to exactly what they need, and nothing more.
  • Implementation partners & VARsDelivery teams work on your systems without data leaving your tenant.
  • Offshore & contract staffControlled access for extended teams, revoked the moment it ends.

Data stays in your tenant

Systems and data live on your Azure. Access happens inside the workspace, so nothing is copied to a laptop you can't see.

Controls against exfiltration

Copy, download, clipboard and print policies tuned per group, so access does not become a leak.

Right person, right window

Role-based, time-boxed access for auditors, partners and contractors, granted and revoked cleanly.

Built on AVD & Nerdio

Azure Virtual Desktop for the workspace, Nerdio for management and auto-scaling that keeps compute cost sensible.

Two halves, one design

Hosting and access, designed together

We don't bolt security on afterwards. The cloud your systems run on and the way people reach them are designed as one, so the result is secure by default and properly supportable.
