Selected work

A little of our recent work

A sample of recent engagements across the CFO-CIO bridge. Client names withheld pending approval.

EPM ReplacementAdvisory

Independent advice on an EPM platform replacement

Provided independent advisory support to a global industrial manufacturing group weighing a replacement for its legacy EPM platform. We reviewed the products, helped shape the shortlist, took part in the vendor meetings, and ran a structured comparison of the final three before giving a steer on product fit and the wider technology roadmap, working alongside the client's Business Support team, and through them their technical, hosting and vendor teams.

  • Final three compared: the incumbent and two contenders
  • Assessed on technology, functionality, support, usability, cost, and viability and lifecycle
  • A steer on product fit and the wider technology roadmap
SAP BPC on AzureAdvisory + Assurance

SAP BPC EPM deployment, hosted on the client's own Azure

Supported a UK-listed international manufacturing group across its SAP BPC programme, from helping shape and understand the requirements and RFP and feeding into the evaluation, through to the vision, strategy and business case for deployment on the client's own Azure tenant, delivered by a hosting partner and VAR. We owned the high-level technical design and provided QA and independent advisory throughout, with the client in control of their own environment.

  • Requirements, RFP and evaluation shaped with an independent eye
  • High-level solution design owned independently of the vendor
  • Hosted on the client's own Azure, with QA protecting the business case
