How we work
A Clear, Structured Approach

We follow a proven four-step process so there are no surprises — just a well-delivered outcome.

1

CPM/EPM Strategy & Readiness

We start by understanding how finance actually operates today — planning cycles, consolidation and close processes, reporting hierarchies, data lineage, and where the pain really lives. We assess whether the underlying problem is process, data, or technology (often it's not the tool). This gives you a clear-eyed view of what "good" looks like before any vendor conversation begins.

2

Vendor Selection

We run a structured, independent selection process across the leading CPM/EPM platforms — Anaplan, OneStream, Oracle EPM, SAP, and others — matched against your actual requirements, not a generic checklist. We manage RFPs, demos, and reference calls, and we give you a clear, defensible recommendation. You choose the platform; we make sure it's the right choice for the right reasons.

3

Target Operating Model & Process Design

Before implementation starts, we design how finance will actually run on the new platform — process flows, controls, ownership, data governance, and reporting structure. This is the step most projects skip, and it's the single biggest predictor of whether an implementation succeeds or becomes shelfware.

4

Implementation Assurance

Whether we deliver the build ourselves or you engage a specialist implementation partner, we stay engaged as your independent quality check — reviewing design decisions, holding the delivery team to the agreed process design, and protecting the business case we helped you build. You're never negotiating alone with the people building the system.
