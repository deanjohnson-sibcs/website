WHAT IS AZURE VIRTUAL DESKTOP?
A full Windows desktop, delivered from the cloud

Azure Virtual Desktop (AVD) is Microsoft's cloud-hosted desktop and application service. Instead of running Windows on a physical PC, your users connect to a Windows desktop running securely in Microsoft Azure — from any device, anywhere in the world.

Work from anywhere

Users get a full Windows 11 desktop experience on any device — laptop, tablet, thin client, or even a personal PC — without compromising security or performance.

Enterprise-grade security

Data never leaves Azure. Combined with Microsoft Entra ID, Conditional Access, and Intune, AVD gives you complete control over who accesses what — and from where.

Multi-session Windows

Multiple users can share a single virtual machine simultaneously, dramatically reducing infrastructure costs compared to traditional VDI or physical desktops.

Seamless Microsoft 365 integration

AVD is built for Microsoft 365 — with optimised Teams calls, OneDrive integration, and single sign-on across all your Microsoft apps out of the box.

Scale up or down instantly

Need 10 desktops today and 200 next month? AVD scales in minutes. You only pay for what you use — no hardware procurement, no lead times.

Reduce hardware costs

Extend the life of existing devices, eliminate refresh cycles, and reduce capital expenditure — your compute power lives in Azure, not on a desk.

WHO IT'S FOR
AVD is a great fit if you…

Have remote or hybrid workers

Give home workers the same secure, consistent desktop as office staff — no VPN complexity, no performance compromise.

Moving premises or replacing ageing hardware? Shift to cloud desktops and remove the hardware dependency entirely.
