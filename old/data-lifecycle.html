THE CHALLENGE
Data without governance is a liability

Most organisations have a data problem — and a governance problem on top of it. Reports conflict, nobody knows where data came from, sensitive information lives in the wrong places, and compliance teams are constantly firefighting.

Data scattered everywhere

Multiple systems, spreadsheets, and databases — no single version of the truth, and no way to know which number to trust.

No visibility of sensitive data

Personal data, financial records, and confidential information spread across the estate — with no clear map of where it is or who can see it.

Unknown data lineage

When a report shows an unexpected number, nobody can trace it back to the source — so decisions are made on figures nobody fully trusts.

Compliance & audit risk

GDPR, FCA, ISO 27001 — without a governed data catalogue, demonstrating compliance requires enormous manual effort every time.

Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and Purview solve this together — giving you a governed data lake, automated pipelines, a living data catalogue, and AI-powered reports your whole organisation can trust.

THE PLATFORM
Four layers. One connected platform.

Fabric, Purview, and Power BI cover every stage of the data journey — from ingestion through governance to insight — without the complexity of stitching together separate tools.

Layer 1 — Store

OneLake

All data from every source lands in one governed lake. No copies, no silos, one version of truth.

Connect any source

ERP, CRM, databases, files, cloud services — Fabric connects without complex integration work.

Unified security

Access controls applied once in OneLake, enforced everywhere — across every tool and every workload.

Layer 2 — Transform

Automated pipelines

Data moved, cleaned, and shaped on a schedule — reports always reflect the latest position.

Lakehouse & warehouse

Combine the flexibility of a data lake with the structure of a warehouse — at scale.

AI-assisted preparation

Copilot in Fabric helps generate pipelines and transformations using natural language.

Layer 3 — Govern

Microsoft Purview

A living data catalogue — every dataset scanned, classified, and mapped so your team knows exactly what data exists and where.

Sensitivity labels & DLP

Automatically classify and label sensitive data. Policies prevent oversharing before it happens.

Data lineage

Trace any number in any report back to its source — end to end — in seconds.

Layer 4 — Report

Power BI

Beautiful, interactive dashboards built on governed data — trusted by the whole business, on any device.

Real-time refresh

Reports update automatically — leaders get up-to-the-minute visibility without manual exports.

Copilot for Power BI

Ask questions in plain English — instant answers, summaries, and visuals. No SQL required.

Why governance matters before you report: Copilot and Power BI surface data from across your Microsoft 365 and Fabric environment. Without Purview classifying and protecting that data first, AI-powered reports can expose sensitive information to the wrong people — instantly, at scale.

Microsoft Purview — Data Governance

Know your data. Protect your data. Prove your compliance.

Microsoft Purview is the governance layer that makes the rest of the platform trustworthy. It scans your entire data estate — in Fabric, Microsoft 365, and beyond — cataloguing what exists, classifying what's sensitive, mapping where it flows, and enforcing policies that keep it safe.

Unified data catalogue

A searchable, living inventory of every data asset across your organisation — so anyone can find the right dataset and understand what it contains.

Automatic classification

Purview scans your data and automatically applies sensitivity labels — personal data, financial records, intellectual property — without manual effort.

End-to-end data lineage

See exactly how every dataset was created, transformed, and used — from source system to Power BI report — so you can trust every number.

Data Loss Prevention

Policies automatically prevent sensitive data from being shared inappropriately — in emails, Teams, SharePoint, and Power BI reports alike.

Compliance made demonstrable

GDPR, FCA, ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials — Purview gives you the audit trail and evidence pack regulators and auditors need, without manual assembly.

Data estate visibility

A map of your entire data landscape — what exists, where it lives, who owns it, and how it's classified — maintained automatically as your data grows.

Under the hood — for your IT & data team
Data Map
Automated scanning & registration

Purview's Data Map scans registered sources on a schedule — populating the catalogue with schema, classifications, and lineage automatically.

Information Protection
Sensitivity labels across Microsoft 365

Labels defined once in Purview are enforced across Office files, SharePoint, Teams, Power BI, and Fabric — with encryption and access restrictions applied automatically.

Data Lifecycle Management
Retention & disposition policies

Automatically retain data that must be kept and dispose of data that shouldn't be — reducing storage costs and compliance risk simultaneously.

eDiscovery & Audit
Investigation & audit trail

Unified audit logs and eDiscovery tools let you investigate incidents, respond to subject access requests, and demonstrate compliance to regulators.

Data Lineage
Fabric & Power BI lineage integration

Purview natively integrates with Fabric and Power BI — automatically mapping lineage from pipeline source through to published report without manual configuration.

Access Policies
Purview access policies for OneLake

Define and enforce fine-grained data access policies directly from Purview — controlling who can read or modify data in Fabric without touching Azure RBAC.
