Performance & optimisation
The impact of BPC performance tuning

Poorly optimised BPC environments are common — accumulated technical debt, inefficient logic, and suboptimal data models compound over time. Our performance tuning engagements consistently deliver significant improvements.

70%
Typical reduction in script logic run time

Refactoring inefficient UJFS and script logic — eliminating unnecessary loops, redundant calculations, and hardcoded member references — typically reduces execution time by 50–80%.

10×

Migrating from flat-file based Data Manager packages to native HANA-optimised data flows can reduce data load times by an order of magnitude for large datasets.

Days → Hours
Consolidation cycle time reduction

Optimising consolidation logic, intercompany elimination rules, and currency translation routines regularly reduces end-to-end consolidation run time from days to hours or less.

Zero
Unplanned downtime after stabilisation

Rescue and stabilisation engagements consistently eliminate the recurring processing failures, data corruption events, and unplanned outages that disrupt month-end and planning cycles.

Key migration considerations
Plan early — migrations take time

Complex BPC migrations typically take 6–18 months. Starting before your maintenance deadline gives you options; starting after removes them.

Historical data strategy

Deciding how much history to migrate, what to archive, and what to leave behind is one of the most important early decisions in any BPC migration.

Parallel running

Running old and new environments in parallel for at least one full planning or close cycle is essential for validation and stakeholder confidence.

Business change, not just technical change

A platform migration is also a process change. Planning for user training, process redesign, and stakeholder communication is as important as the technical work.
